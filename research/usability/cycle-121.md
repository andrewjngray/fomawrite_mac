# Cycle 121 — optional review

- Choose View → Title Bar → Always Show. Type and scroll either pane: both bars remain visible.
- Choose Fade In/Out. Type or scroll: bars fade. Reveal either edge, move away: that bar stays visible until the next edit or scroll.
- Check the mutually exclusive menu checkmarks and synchronized Auto-Hide Document Bars checkbox. Close/reopen the app and confirm your choice remains.
