# Cycle 9 usability check

- Move the mouse quickly across file rows: paths should not flash up.
- Rest over one filename for about 0.7 seconds: its full path should appear; moving away should dismiss it.
- Click Previews beside Sort by: excerpts should appear. Click again for icon-and-filename rows.
- Check Show Text Excerpts in Sort by stays synchronized; optionally enable Show Date.
- Restart the app and confirm your selected display options persist.
- Report whether the delay feels right and whether Previews is clear enough as a label.
