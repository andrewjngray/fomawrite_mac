# Cycle 81 review — export layout

In Fomawrite Dev, open Export at your normal window size. The built-in style heading and every card should start at the same left edge, and the slim scrollbar should stay in its own gutter to the right. Scroll to Your styles and confirm Name this copy and Duplicate current fit fully. Narrow the main window toward its minimum: the export dialog should stay within it, hide the live preview, and let you scroll through output, styles, paper and orientation without clipping the Save and Cancel actions.
