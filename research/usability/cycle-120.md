# Cycle 120 — optional review

- Scroll to hide both bars. Reveal the top bar, then move back into the document: it should stay visible.
- Reveal the bottom bar and move away: both should stay visible. Click inside publishing: no hiding.
- Type in Source or Visual Edit, or scroll either pane: both bars should hide again. Repeat once.
- Open a footer menu and move through it; controls should remain reachable. Turn off View → Auto-Hide Document Bars for always-visible bars.
- Compare Single/Split and Source/Visual Edit: the editing pane and its bars should be soft grey, with a warm cream publishing surround. Switching or hiding bars must not move the divider or content.
