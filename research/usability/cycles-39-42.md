# RC1 personal acceptance exercise

- Open Closeout.md from research/cycle-41/sample. Review spelling, replace one word and Undo. Try your preferred installed language with a disposable sample.
- Command-Shift-P → Paginated Preview. Check both pages and fit controls. Try the supplied long-note sample and return links.
- Mark/copy a sentence, inspect labels, and test pasting through the other writing app you use.
- Hover a folder for two seconds; resize to minimum width; traverse important controls by keyboard.
- Save, enter fullscreen, Quit and relaunch. With an external display available, check placement after disconnecting it.

Record feedback and hardware results in docs/release-acceptance.md. Do not run forced shutdown/device-removal tests on important writing. RC1 has not received final parity/accessibility/distribution sign-off.
