# Cycle 105 — cleaner native toolbar

Open **Fomawrite Dev.app**, build **105**.

1. Compare the toolbar with your Ulysses reference: darker glyphs, quieter circles, one uninterrupted formatting capsule, simpler sidebar/compose/link/share symbols and a solid paragraph mark.
2. Select a Source phrase, use Bold and Italic, then Undo. Open the paragraph and link controls; dismiss without changing the document.
3. Try the blue folder heading, − / + and Aa. They retain their existing actions.
4. Collapse/reopen both navigation panes, narrow the window and try Dark appearance. Check that the simpler controls remain recognizable and easy to click.
